The Photographer

Chasing light
through every frame

I am a documentary and travel photographer drawn to the quiet in-between moments — the pause before laughter, the shadow after light, the silence a landscape holds at dusk.

My work spans the ghats of Varanasi, the high passes of Ladakh, the monsoon corridors of Coorg — places where the soul of India speaks in texture and tone.

I believe photography is not about capturing what exists, but about revealing what we feel in its presence.
